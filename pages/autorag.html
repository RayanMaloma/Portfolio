AutoRAG (Graduation Project)

For King Saud University
Role UI/UX · Frontend Developer
Status In Progress

Overview

AutoRAG is a user-friendly AI system that allows users to ask high-level questions without technical knowledge. The system automatically selects the most suitable RAG configuration to provide accurate and contextually relevant answers.

What to explore

  • 01 Guided RAG setup A no-code questionnaire configures the engine for you
  • 02 Natural-language chat Ask high-level questions, get sourced answers
  • 03 Document workspaces Upload, index and manage knowledge sources
  • 04 Team & insights Members, roles and usage analytics
