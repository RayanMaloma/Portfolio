# Case-Study Content Requirements
_Phase 5 companion — 2026-07-17. The single biggest quality lever of the redesign. Short honest answers beat polished long ones; bullet points are fine — I write the final prose, you approve it. Skip any question that doesn't apply, write "n/a" rather than inventing._

## Questions for ALL three projects (AutoRAG · D Shopper · CSC)

**Context**
1. One sentence: what is it and who is it for?
2. When did you work on it (months/years) and how long?
3. Who else was involved (team size, roles) and what was *exclusively* yours?
4. What was the starting point — a brief, an idea, an existing product?

**Problem & constraints**
5. What was broken/missing/hard for users before?
6. Top 3 constraints (time, tech, stakeholders, skills, budget)?
7. Who were the actual users, and how did you learn anything about them (even informally — classmates testing counts, say so honestly)?

**Process**
8. Walk me through your steps roughly in order (research → flows → wireframes → UI → testing → handoff, whatever it actually was).
9. What process artifacts still exist? (Figma files, wireframes, flows, sketches, early ugly versions — early ugly versions are GOLD for credibility. Screenshots welcome.)
10. What did you try that didn't work?

**Key decisions (the heart of the template — aim for 2–4 each)**
11. Name a design decision you fought for or agonized over. What were the options, what did you pick, why?
12. What did you deliberately NOT do / cut?

**Outcome**
13. What shipped / what state is it in now?
14. Any numbers at all: users, grade, stakeholder feedback, completion rate, "the club used it for X months", "professor said Y"? Honest proxies beat fake metrics.
15. What would you do differently now?
16. One sentence: what did this project teach you that you used in the next one?
