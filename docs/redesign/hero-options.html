About the photos you sent

Honest photo assessment

  • Desk/lamp photo (Option 1): the mood is exactly right for the Juan treatment. It appears to be AI-generated or heavily AI-stylized (it arrived as a ChatGPT image file). My strong recommendation: don't ship an AI-generated portrait as the face of a personal portfolio — if a reviewer suspects it, it undermines exactly the authenticity a portfolio exists to prove. Recreating this exact setup as a real photo is easy (desk, warm lamp, laptop, shot from slightly below) and I'd add it to the shoot list.
  • Night selfie (Option 2): right framing instincts, but it's a low-angle night phone shot with mixed street light — usable as a stand-in only. Pose 1/3 from the brief replaces it.
  • Traditional-dress portrait (Option 3): genuinely strong — professional light, rich texture, real presence. If you like Option 3's direction, a version of this looking at or near the camera would be the finished asset; the current side-glance also works if we lean editorial.
  • Campfire photo (IMG_2776, fur hat): not hero material, but it's a perfect About-page personality shot — warm, candid, memorable. Earmarked for the About "personal layer" section.
